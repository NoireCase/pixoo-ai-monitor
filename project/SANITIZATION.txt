Sanitized source distribution v0.2.0
Runtime configuration, credential stores, logs, heartbeat files, backups and real account images are excluded.
docs/screens PNGs are generated solely from synthetic data by scripts/generate_readme_previews.py; they contain no live account values or device photos.
Original 16x16 screens are production Renderer outputs. Labels outside the screens explicitly identify demo data.
Text B2 golden masks are test fixtures, not live screenshots.
Project MIT license and upstream PixooCtrl MIT notice preserved.
